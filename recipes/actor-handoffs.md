# Actor handoffs

Use when a consequential boundary can develop from an object, text group or container already participating in the explanation. These are reference-grounded choreography examples. [motion-direction](../references/motion-direction.md) owns continuity, rhythm, reading and material limits; [asset-routing](../references/asset-routing.md) owns implementation selection; [quality](../references/quality.md) owns review. Resolve the look through the effective project `frame.md`.

## Choose by the next visible consequence

| The next idea requires… | Useful choreography |
| --- | --- |
| Inspect several parts, then understand their arrangement | Track the parts, then gather and reframe |
| Make a principle dominant, then expose its explanation | Pass through a headline's foreground |
| Read evidence, then compare its important values | Extract evidence into diagram actors |
| Use existing contents in a new operation | Release and regroup contained objects |
| Leave one environment and reveal another | Carry a topic-related prop across the boundary |
| Turn a question or detail into a working interface | Let the receiving container take over the view |

These choices can combine. Select the route that makes the particular relationship visible. A fixed reading view or clear cut remains available under motion-direction.

## Track, gather and reframe

**Observed:** [video-production workflow](https://www.douyin.com/video/7690144052951436607), approximately 3–13.2s. The view travels among cards, settles on a subject, visits process cards, then retreats to expose their sequence. The cards shrink upward while a repository panel arrives below. Separate planes, changing occlusion and soft background cards support depth; the source does not establish the original renderer or camera parameters.

**Author:** Place the parts according to their relationship. Use one traceable route to visit them, then change both their grouping and the framing so the whole becomes legible. Preserve the visited parts at their new scale; reduce their internal detail where appropriate. The incoming overview or parent can form during the retreat instead of appearing after every part disappears.

**Material:** Planar objects support regrouping and reframing. An angled gallery needs coherent planes and an existing perspective rig. Obtain actual geometry or multiple views when the route must reveal an object's other side.

**Evidence to inspect:** The overview explains where the visited parts ended up; a retreat reveals an arrangement rather than only making the same panel smaller.

## Headline foreground passage

**Observed:** [Agent work boundaries](https://www.douyin.com/video/7685681479640272180), approximately 12–18s. Dark comparison cards give way to an oversized “工作边界” heading. Its cropped glyphs cross the view before the heading settles for reading. It grows again and moves out as a field of files arrives underneath.

**Author:** Give the principle a readable focal state. Let a large glyph group or its associated surface occupy the foreground during the handoff, then carry that group beyond the view while the explanatory objects arrive. Size the passage from actual glyph bounds and the intended landing. A decisive near pass can exceed the viewport; it is a different task from a small detail zoom.

**Material:** Outlined text or separately controlled glyph/group wrappers can provide stable geometry. Use the selected project's typography and material behavior; the reference's typeface and palette are not part of the motion recipe.

**Evidence to inspect:** The travel produces the promised near pass, the heading has a readable window, and its departure leads attention to the explanation.

## Extract evidence into actors

**Observed:** [WorkBuddy interface and backend values](https://www.douyin.com/video/7671969146573212968), approximately 5.8–13.8s. The bill panel recedes while highlighted values remain as separate comparison cards. Their containers turn away; the values take over the dark field, and the calculation appears before the next row comparison forms.

**Author:** Identify the exact source region, then carry its value or concise claim into a diagram role. Keep its projected position understandable while the source recedes, its container changes or its counterpart arrives. Move from evidence → extracted actor → explanatory relationship, with the relevant source label retained. A derived value must follow the supplied calculation and preserve units and qualifications.

**Material:** A screenshot plus an independently rendered text/value actor is sufficient. Use a cropped source region when reproducing its exact appearance matters. Show the overlay-to-actor handoff at a matching position and scale, then hand that actor to the next layout.

**Evidence to inspect:** The viewer can identify which evidence supplied the new actor. Cropping or redrawing retains the actual value and context; retain the actual source context.

## Release and regroup contents

**Observed:** [underestimated Chatbot](https://www.douyin.com/video/7674835609818615092), approximately 5.25–6.25s. Three image thumbnails leave a lower chat container and move into the upper area while the tool thumbnail stays below. The images themselves carry the transition between arrangements.

**Author:** Give contents their own controllable groups. Lift the selected members out of the original container, maintain their recognizable order or identity as required by the claim, then land them in the receiving arrangement. The old container may become context while the contents carry the new focus.

**Material:** Separate image crops or SVG groups. When changing parent wrappers would change coordinates, preserve the current projected transform at the handoff or animate the existing wrapper into the receiving layout.

**Evidence to inspect:** The same members can be followed across the container boundary.

## Topic-related prop passage

**Observed:** [tools that are too noisy](https://www.douyin.com/video/7664150164537363738), approximately 7–10.2s. A labeled parcel/bag grows from a cupboard area toward the foreground, becomes soft near the view, then clears left as the file-list environment appears to its right. The prop's approach and departure conceal the environmental change.

**Author:** Choose a prop that belongs to the explanation. Bring its silhouette toward the foreground, use its covered region for the scene handoff, and continue its motion as the next target becomes clear. Keep passage direction comprehensible and place the new reading view after the prop clears.

**Material:** A transparent PNG or SVG supports a planar silhouette pass. Use additional drawings or video if the prop must visibly change shape, open or turn. Apply supported photographic blur/reveal treatments through the existing media-use route in asset-routing; graphic leaf-layer focus remains governed by motion-direction.

**Evidence to inspect:** The source scene remains visible in uncovered regions until the authored handoff; the prop clears the new focal subject and captions. A full-screen blur does not demonstrate a prop passage.

## Receiving container takeover

**Observed:** [haircut planning tool](https://www.douyin.com/video/7650450947726946559), approximately 2.8–9.8s. Floating comments establish a question; a phone frame rises around that field and takes over the composition. Its interface fills in, then the view approaches the relevant details while the outer phone remains identifiable.

**Author:** Establish the question or content field, introduce the receiving frame around it, then develop the interface or mechanism inside that container. Reframe toward the required detail after the relationship is established. A wrapper or mask can perform the takeover; an orbit is unnecessary when no unseen side is needed.

**Material:** A controllable frame/mask and independently timed contents. A static full screenshot supports a planar push-in; detailed interaction needs authored interface states or source footage.

**Evidence to inspect:** The container's arrival explains the shift from question to working view. The required content fits its eventual viewport and remains readable after the approach.

## Use the existing storyboard and checks

Write the selected sequence in the existing beat/boundary entry under motion-direction, including the actor's old → new role only when it changes. For example: “the measured value leaves the evidence panel, holds its projected location while the panel recedes, then joins the comparison; source label remains beside it.” Link the real implementation and the reference range there. Use the existing continuity channels instead of adding a second camera plan or transition schema.

First implement the smallest representative span that includes the difficult handoff and its reading view. Select native HyperFrames primitives by the required behavior; author a project path when the native choices do not fit. Use the existing transform owners and composition clock. Review departure, passage, arrival and normal-speed readability through quality, then replace the content and recompute the layout before promoting reusable coverage under release-scope.
