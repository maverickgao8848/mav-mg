# Protective surface

Components: `interface-support`

Use when a supported object or phase becomes a boundary layer that changes conditions beneath it.

1. Begin from the settled subject and interface anchors of `interface-support`.
2. Expand the subject laterally with HyperFrames `center-outward-expansion` while keeping the interface fixed.
3. Reveal the protected region from the boundary downward with transform-only motion.
4. Introduce subordinate life or system elements after the layer is established.
5. Hold the final relationship: surface layer, protected region and consequence label.

The layer’s protective effect must come from the supplied explanation. Do not invent temperatures, rates or magnitudes.
